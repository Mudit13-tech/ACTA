# Fonts

Drop your licensed HV Florentino web files here, named exactly:

    Florentino.woff2
    Florentino.woff        (optional, older browsers)

They are picked up automatically by the `@font-face` rule at the top of
`src/design.css`. Until they exist the app falls back to Cormorant Garamond,
a Renaissance serif of similar colour and contrast, so nothing looks broken.

HV Florentino is a commercial typeface by Harmonais Visual — buy a webfont
licence from Fontspring, MyFonts or Creative Market.
